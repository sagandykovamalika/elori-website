Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Elori ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Elori iOS application (the "App") or visit tryelori.com and its subdomains (the "Website" and, together with the App, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

A. Photos, Videos, and Creations

Elori requests access to the photos and videos you choose to use and permission to save finished creations to your photo library. Editing, rendering, background removal, and project storage are performed on your device. Your personal photos, videos, and saved projects are not uploaded to Elori's servers, are not reviewed by us, and are not used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

If you use Photo Ideas, the App analyzes eligible photos on your device to suggest projects. This analysis may use photo creation dates, coarse location metadata already attached to photos, favorite status, dimensions, camera metadata, detected faces, visual labels, and aesthetic scores. Elori stores a bounded cache of thumbnails and derived analysis on your device using iOS file protection. This information is not uploaded to Elori. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple to your device so the App can process it.

B. App Usage and Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. It may collect a Firebase app-instance or installation identifier, device and operating system information, app version, language, approximate region derived from IP address, and events such as onboarding progress, paywall views, purchase or restore outcomes, exports, shares, and the identifiers of App-provided templates or design assets used. We do not send the contents of your personal photos, videos, or projects as analytics events. Advertising storage, ad-user-data, and ad-personalization consent are disabled in the App.

C. Crash and Diagnostic Information

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash traces, diagnostic and installation identifiers, timestamps, App state, App version, and device and operating system information. We use this information to diagnose crashes and improve stability.

D. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. We use Adapty to display paywall offerings, validate purchases, manage access to paid features, and measure subscription performance. Adapty creates an anonymous profile for an App installation and may process an internal profile identifier, an identifier for the device or App installation, device and App information, App Store country, paywall interactions, and purchase and subscription history. Elori also provides Adapty with the Firebase app-instance identifier so subscription events can be measured consistently. Elori does not require an account and does not provide Adapty with your name, email address, or an Elori account ID.

E. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging process your APNs device token and a Firebase installation or registration token to deliver messages. Firebase may also process device model, language, time zone, operating system, App identifier and version, and notification delivery or interaction information. Notification content may contain an internal template or collection identifier so the App can open the requested content.

F. Content Delivery and Network Data

Elori uses Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Google Cloud Storage, and an image cache to download App-provided templates, fonts, stickers, frames, examples, configuration, and other creative assets. These services may process IP address, request timestamps, App or installation identifiers, and content-delivery and network metadata needed to respond to requests and protect the Service.

G. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The App stores projects and their images, previews, Photo Ideas analysis, preferences, onboarding and review state, recent selections, entitlement state, cached App assets, and diagnostic logs locally on your device. This information remains there until it is removed by the App or iOS, you delete it in the App where that option is available, or you uninstall the App.

H. Website Measurement

The Website uses the OpenAI Ads Measurement Pixel to measure visits and clicks from the Website to Elori's App Store listing and to attribute those actions to an eligible ChatGPT ad. The Pixel may process the page origin, event name and timestamp, an OpenAI ad-click reference, and a randomly generated browser reference. It may store first-party cookies named __oppref for up to 30 days and __obref for up to 365 days. Elori's implementation does not provide the Pixel with names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other customer-account information.

I. Information You Provide

If you contact us for support, submit feedback, or otherwise communicate with us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic report or attachment you decide to send.

2. How We Use Information

We use information described above to:

  • Provide editing, project, export, subscription, notification, and content-delivery features.
  • Remember preferences and maintain access to paid features.
  • Understand feature use and improve the App and Website.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, security incidents, and performance problems.
  • Measure advertising attribution and App Store clicks.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. When We Disclose Information

We disclose information only as described in this Policy:

  • Service providers. Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide analytics, crash reporting, messaging, databases, storage, and content delivery; Adapty provides paywall and subscription management; Apple provides photo-library access, iCloud photo retrieval, notifications, StoreKit, and App Store payment processing; and OpenAI provides Website ad measurement.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, Elori, or the Service.
  • Business transfers. Information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information for money. Website measurement information is sent to OpenAI for advertising attribution as described above; depending on where you live, applicable law may define this disclosure as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Third-Party Privacy Practices

Our providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Adapty Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

We retain information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the Service, supporting users, meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes, and protecting the Service. Retention periods vary by data type and provider. Google states that Firebase Crashlytics generally retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. Website Pixel cookies request the lifetimes listed in Section 1.H, although browser settings may shorten them. Apple, Google, Adapty, and OpenAI retain information according to their applicable policies and service settings.

On-device projects, preferences, caches, and analysis remain until removed as described in Section 1.G. Support correspondence is retained as needed to respond to you and maintain appropriate business records.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Use iOS Settings to grant limited photo access, change photo permissions, or disable notifications.
  • Use the App's controls to remove individual projects where available, or uninstall the App to remove its locally stored data.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Block or clear Website cookies using your browser settings.
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control, or to object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.

Because Elori does not require an account, we may need information from you to locate records associated with a request. Apple manages payment, refund, cancellation, and billing records, so some requests must be made through your Apple ID or Apple Support. You may also have the right to appeal our response or complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. International Processing

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they or their service providers operate. Those countries may have data protection laws different from those where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including on-device processing and iOS file protection for cached Photo Ideas analysis. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. We will post the revised Policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or by other appropriate means.
